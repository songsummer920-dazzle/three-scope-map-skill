# MapLibre basemap with geographic 3D overlay

Select `overlay` when roads, water, labels and extruded administrative regions must remain visible together. The existing `hybrid` switch remains a different composition: independent renderers sharing administrative navigation.

Use the bundled `VectorMap.vue`, `vectorMap3dLayer.ts` and `vectorMap3dGeometry.ts` inside the selected template family. MapLibre owns the canvas, GL context, camera, navigation and repaint lifecycle. In FUI, both `terrain-glass` and `smooth-glass` delegate to `FuiMapSharedGlassLayer` and `fuiMapGeographicGlass.ts`: their Three.js `WebGLRenderer` is a state adapter around MapLibre's existing context, not another canvas or context. It creates no OrbitControls or independent RAF. The classic overlay retains its existing custom-layer material path.

```vue
<VectorMap
  :active="true"
  :overlay="true"
  overlay-surface="smooth-glass"
  primary-color="#A75FFF"
  quality="balanced"
/>
```

The common navigation adapter supplies complete real current-scope GeoJSON to the 3D layer, including `_JD` line features. Keep the ordinary 2D fill filter separate: stripping `_JD` before the native 3D builder removes its South China Sea segments. The geographic builder excludes `_JD` from administrative fills and region picking, then builds those original segments through the dedicated China-only maritime layer. Preserve holes, disconnected polygons and antimeridian handling. Convert longitude/latitude to real Mercator coordinates and express height in geographic metres; a display extrusion is not a measured DEM elevation. `terrain-glass` reuses the standalone terrain texture and relief factories with an exact Mercator geographic adapter. Its administrative slab height remains explicitly a display effect.

## Native FUI maps

The FUI overlay entrance uses MapLibre's shared camera: approach the saved final bearing and pitch with a small rotation over the existing 780 ms map entrance interval. Basemap, geographic volume, lighting projection and picking remain in the same camera; never animate the 3D group or a second canvas independently. Play on the first overlay reveal, on entry from Earth and on explicit replay. Cached resumes, palette/material changes and drilldown do not replay the entrance. Restore the exact final camera on programmatic cancellation; user input takes over at the current pose. Reduced-motion mode shows the final pose immediately. Use MapLibre's own easing/repaint lifecycle rather than another RAF or renderer.

Both FUI overlays retain the standalone 32-unit body thickness, side-gradient factory, unioned beveled exterior walls, native outer/bottom rims, provincial lines and anchored selection lift. Entity `terrain-glass` retains its terrain relief and textured glass surface; hologram `smooth-glass` retains its polished coat/backing pair, geographic reflection and studio PMREM. Keep the smooth 2.6-unit near-top backing gap independent of body thickness. The resting fine line core stays visible; its halo is selection-only. Omit the standalone HUD, compass, ground-wave instrument and diffuse floor/stage. Use native geographic content on the basemap, without a second canvas or a simplified replacement shader. The following optical contract applies to smooth glass.

Entity terrain uses the standalone `createFuiMapSurface` and physical clear coat, direct sRGB rendering and its existing light rig. It does not inherit the hologram's HDR/Bloom output. Both shader UVs and CPU relief picking explicitly invert Mercator latitude; the standalone affine projection remains the default. Start the same regional-relief request as soon as the new geographic projection is known, in parallel with native geometry and texture preparation outside the GL callback. If the height field is already available when the model is prepared, apply its filtered height and regional hit proxies before the replacement's first draw. Otherwise show the correctly scoped overview, prepare both height endpoints outside the draw callback, then blend late visible detail over 380 ms with the existing shared terrain uniform. Height, normals and surface shading follow that same blend; private CPU picking reads the matching interpolated heights, with conservative endpoint bounds and no dense per-frame vertex rewrite. Hidden preparation accepts completed detail at full strength before reveal. Cancel stale requests on scope/material replacement or removal. A network failure retains the overview and reports `terrainDetail="overview"`; detail remains `loading` during its blend and reports `regional` with its actual resolution only at completion. Smooth glass makes no terrain request.

`createFuiGeographicGlassProjection` preserves the standalone map's original 860 × 530, 28-padding longitude unit: `mapScale = legacyLongitudeScale = min((860 - 56) / lngSpan, (530 - 56) / latSpan)` and `scale = 1 / (360 * mapScale)`. Compute spans from the real non-`_JD` geography, retaining short-arc antimeridian handling and finite empty/degenerate inputs. XY remains exact Mercator, not a planar longitude/latitude fit. `T(anchor + worldWrap) * S(scale, -scale, scale)` maps native coordinates back into Mercator; the sign change converts north-positive native Y to south-positive Mercator Y. Native z44–z12 thickness and the 2.6-unit engraving gap therefore retain their standalone proportions and map to display metres at that geographic anchor. Preserve this explicit anchor-metre meaning instead of labeling extrusion as measured terrain.

`fuiHologramGlassOptics.ts` supplies the shared Earth/map linear-radiance core: pigment, backing/body absorption, lip, coverage and 36/20 key reflection. Earth and map retain their geometry-specific adapters: the map retains its 2.6-unit near-top backing and 32-unit exterior body.

The shared map adapter adds one subdued colored provincial boundary image on the existing backing. Keep the actual backing gap at 2.6 units. Its calibrated virtual reach beneath that backing is `min(3 * engravingDepth, max(glassDepth - engravingDepth, 0))`, normally 7.8 units, placing the image 10.4 units below the coat. This bounded optical-depth approximation is not an exact geometric mirror or path-traced reflection. Sample along the actual planar Snell view ray in native map units: it aligns at normal incidence and separates with camera angle, rather than following a fixed screen offset. Reuse the boundary atlas, two-way pigment attenuation and one shared skin response; retain the bright top core and existing directional shadow. Gate both the receiving point and sampled source against the national exterior to prevent a doubled country rim. This map-only addition creates no line mesh, render target, context or RAF, and leaves the original Earth cap unchanged.

`fuiMapRegion.ts` keeps 103.6°E/35.2°N only for China country scope (`country:100000`, or the recognized bundled province collection at the low-level API). Other scopes derive their optical center from the current geography with circular-longitude bounds; world views compress only the shading angle. `fuiMapCapOptics.ts` recovers real longitude/latitude in explicit equirectangular/Mercator mode. One fixed initial rest-view frame rotates normal and light together, with `EarthReferenceFrame * inverse(Q)` for environment lookups. It does not follow the camera each frame. These are shading transforms only: source coordinates, actual plane normal, Snell ray, height, holes and hit geometry remain authoritative; no per-skin gain is added.

Both smooth standalone and overlay use `FuiGlassHdrPass` for geography-only composition. High/balanced use the linear HalfFloat target (up to 4× MSAA), Earth Bloom response and one ACES/sRGB output; HUD and basemap are excluded. Low disables reflection, owned-target MSAA and Bloom, caps the glass target at 1024 pixels on its long side/DPR1, and caps the MapLibre provider DPR at1. Higher tiers preserve the provider configuration ceiling. Actual extension and framebuffer checks choose HalfFloat+MSAA, HalfFloat without MSAA, linear RGBA8 without Bloom, then direct locally mapped glass if neither offscreen format works. Fallback is observable and preserves the borrowed target; it is a compatibility path, not identical HDR fidelity or a frame-rate guarantee.


`createFuiMapGeographicGlassAsync` moves the native footprint union and beveled exterior-body construction to a dedicated geometry worker while native texture acquisition runs in parallel. The worker uses the existing geometry algorithms and transfers their positions, normals, indices, groups, bounds and exterior ownership; it does not simplify geography, reduce geometry detail or lower the selected quality. Worker cancellation terminates obsolete work. If workers are unavailable, retain the cooperative construction path. Edge-field rasterization and remaining regional material/model stages use the same output with a main-thread yield budget; several tiny stages may share a turn, while individual stages and driver/program first use can still take longer.

`FuiMapSharedGlassLayer` schedules CPU preparation outside MapLibre render, owns its version/AbortController, disposes stale or aborted partial results, and leaves the previous scene visible until a completed replacement commits inside a draw. Retain old GPU owners through the replacement's first draw and camera transition, preserving shared program references. The retirement queue waits at least 900 ms after that draw and until the MapLibre camera is idle, then releases resource-sized steps within an approximately 4 ms budget per borrowed-context draw. A timer requests a repaint but performs no GL work; further retirement draws are bounded by the remaining queue. A newer committed replacement resets the grace period. Removal or context replacement synchronously drains any unfinished retirement. Pending/error geography is not picked as the new scope. PMREM, target changes and actual GPU draws stay inside the borrowed-context callback; no render callback awaits a build and no independent RAF is created. Inactive preparation uses an explicit `requestPreparationFrame()` permit. It survives an asynchronous replacement build and clears only after a successful draw of the requested ready scope. Readiness waits for the basemap, matching composition/navigation and a real overlay draw at the final resized viewport. Hidden relief completes without a continuous blend loop; settled/inactive views stop requesting frames.

Geographic overlays and textured standalone FUI maps batch additive exterior-wall glint coats by administrative owner while retaining their exact vertices, normals, triangle indices, material response and anchored lift. Additive blending permits this grouping without changing transparency order. Normal-blended colored cores use one `THREE.BatchedMesh` draw list across administrative owners. Retain each original chunk's padded bounding sphere and individual frustum culling; sort its sphere center by the original camera's projected depth with stable creation-order ties, rather than sorting a merged owner's center. Per-instance translation and float lift/selection/opacity metadata preserve the existing anchored-foot deformation, normals and Fresnel response. Hidden original cores remain state proxies for established region updates and are excluded from rendered triangle diagnostics. Three uses `WEBGL_multi_draw` where supported and its shared-buffer fallback otherwise; neither path simplifies geography or guarantees a particular frame rate. The standard presentation retains its compatible construction path.
`fuiGlassCapabilities.ts` checks the real extension and a tiny owned RGBA16F color framebuffer before any PMREM or mirror is created, restoring independent draw/read/renderbuffer bindings. If float color targets are unsupported, skip PMREM generation and Reflector construction entirely; retain the map’s pigment, direct key and local glass equations without the environment lookup. Low also creates no Reflector target. A later compositor allocation downgrade retires/disables float-dependent mirrors before drawing, including the direct path. Normal supported high/balanced instances retain their PMREM/reflection recipe.

### Real basemap transmission

Basemap transmission is an optional internal material contract, `uGlassBackdropStrength`. The shared glass appearance defaults to `0`, and zero strength performs no unused framebuffer capture or transmission blend; preserve the material-energy response. When enabled, after PMREM preparation and restoration of the provider framebuffer, `fuiMapBackdrop.ts` captures lower provider geometry on the GPU before the actual native draw. Refraction uses IOR 1.46 and actual eased body thickness, projects the ray exit with the real camera `P * V`, and samples displaced screen UV. The snapshot is at most 2048 pixels on its long side while viewport uniforms retain the original drawing-buffer dimensions. Resolve MSAA at the original size first, then downsample. No CPU `readPixels`, image readback, extra canvas/context or RAF is involved.

Preserve display-encoded bytes in an RGBA8 `FramebufferTexture` with `NoColorSpace` and linear filtering. The shader applies the sRGB EOTF exactly once to enter linear light before absorption and local display mapping. Do not tag this copy sRGB: allocating `SRGB8_ALPHA8` for a LINEAR/RGBA8 source is rejected by WebGL. During Reflector's virtual-camera draw, temporarily disable the main-view backdrop sampler and restore its state afterward. A capture error records its phase, falls back to the native glass scene, and pauses same-size retries until resize/context recreation; it must not blank the scene.

## China-only South China Sea geometry

Only `country:100000` creates the extra South China Sea layer. Native terrain and smooth standalone/overlay maps use the mainland's z12–44 datums for both islands and maritime segments. Their side gradients, bottom edges and anchored selection lift follow the same full 32-unit body thickness. Smooth islands retain their coat/backing recipe (local gap1.9), optical frame and optional backdrop binding, and their pick/lift/highlight belongs to Hainan. The older standard presentation retains its separate shallow models. The fixed screen-space inset remains a standalone-China presentation element; no China inset or maritime model is injected into other countries or regional scopes. These heights and minimum display extents are cartographic exaggeration, not DEM measurements.

Consolidate the island body's opaque cap ranges and transparent side ranges separately, preserving each material queue's exact triangle order. Their existing opaque/transparent render queues already separate those ranges. Maritime cap and side materials are both transparent, so retain their original interleaving rather than regrouping them on that assumption. Preserve coordinates, normals, UVs, holes, display levels and all contour/picking geometry.

## Camera, interaction and layers

- MapLibre owns pan, zoom, bearing and pitch. The FUI smooth adapter reconstructs a rigid Three camera and true eye position from its real projection matrix `P`, Mercator main matrix `M` and local-to-Mercator transform. `vectorMapSharedCamera.ts` removes the shared view scale and fits the projection back to the exact clip XY/Z, including padding and roll. Do not put `M` into a projection-only identity camera: glass view directions and Reflector cameras require the real eye/view.
- The custom layer uses `renderingMode="3d"` and participates in the shared depth buffer. Keep all ordinary provider geometry below it, including provider boundary/road lines interleaved with labels. Raise only symbol layers above it, preserving their relative order. Restore the captured original provider layer order when returning to 2D.
- Map views with this layer use Mercator projection. An omitted provider projection means Mercator; preserve the prior provider projection when leaving overlay mode.
- The initial overlay uses a 50° pitch. At national China scope, fit the mainland/Hainan/Taiwan overview without removing southern-island geometry; users can pan to the complete geography. Keep an already adjusted camera and the original standalone 2D camera behavior.
- Picking follows the displayed raised top surface, including feature lift, holes and the shared Hainan island pick mesh. Pending/error replacement geography is not treated as the new scope. A region click produces one navigation action; disable duplicate2D fill-layer clicks while3D is active.
- During an established overlay's drilldown/back navigation, retain the current camera until a real frame has drawn the matching replacement scope. Fit the destination and emit its navigation event only after that frame; cancel obsolete waits on a newer navigation or removal. This prevents zooming the previous region's texture into the destination. Standalone 2D keeps its existing camera path.
- An intentional hover may prefetch only the next level's administrative JSON after a short dwell. Cancel that intent on pointer exit, navigation or deactivation. Do not build models, request DEM or perform GPU work merely because the pointer crosses a region.
- Suspend fine terrain hover raycasts while the MapLibre camera is moving. Resume settled picking against the displayed geometry; do not add a full refined-terrain raycast to every dragging or easing frame.
- Hover highlights and lifts the whole owned block, with an anchored lower foot. Bound its repaint animation and stop requesting frames when settled or inactive.
- Hue, data and material changes update the existing map/layer. Changing a primary must not reset center, zoom, bearing, pitch or the administrative path.

During navigation, keep the visible breadcrumb on the rendered scope until the replacement has drawn. Show the pending region and a small delayed spinner beside that breadcrumb, with `aria-busy` and a live status for assistive technology. Do not cover the map with a centered loading box. The indicator does not receive pointer input and honors reduced motion; failures use a separate error message.

## Ownership and cleanup

Keep the geographic data, provider adapter, semantic palette and custom rendering layer separate. `onRemove` releases owned buffers/programs/textures and event listeners; it never destroys MapLibre's shared context or canvas. Restore GL bindings/state changed by the custom draw. Handle context loss/restoration without retaining dead GL resources. Suppress stale asynchronous texture callbacks after removal.

For native smooth glass, the layer owns PMREM, reflection/source targets, composer/Bloom/output passes and optional backdrop texture/resolve/downsample FBOs. Regions borrow shared resources. Wall batches also own their aggregate geometry and matrix/index/color metadata textures; call `BatchedMesh.dispose()` once and avoid disposing its aggregate geometry separately. Cancel versioned CPU tasks and dispose stale/partial results on replacement, removal and context loss. Commit completed geography inside a draw and retire the old model in the camera-idle resource steps described above. The model's synchronous `dispose()` must also finish a partially consumed disposal iterator, so removal/context replacement releases every owner even when no further frame is drawn. No asynchronous CPU job calls the borrowed renderer. Standalone shader warm-up still uses `FuiRendererJobs` and exact target variants; do not describe the overlay's worker/cooperative CPU build as eliminating all synchronous GPU/program first-use work.

The MapLibre framebuffer is borrowed: never clear or dispose it, resize its canvas, or call `forceContextLoss`. Use `autoClear=false`; offscreen clears belong only to owned targets. Preserve incoming framebuffer/viewport and pixel-transfer state. A non-null incoming framebuffer needs the scoped return bridge covering target-allocation probes, capability fallback, Reflector and output draws: when Three returns to its `null` target, rebind the borrowed framebuffer through Three's state cache before remaining draws, then restore the method/state. Request antialiasing when MapLibre creates its context; a later Three wrapper cannot add AA to that borrowed context. The owned HDR target's multisampling is a separate setting.

The FUI vector canvas requests `preserveDrawingBuffer:true` at creation so the native physical-transmission result remains available when the shared map settles. Later 2D/overlay switches retain this same context. Expose its actual value as `contextPreserveDrawingBuffer`; verify static frames as well as active rotation. This retains a drawing buffer rather than running a continuous repaint loop, with the corresponding browser buffer-retention cost. Earth, standalone 3D and the classic template keep their own context settings.

An initial overlay frame does not wait for a standalone 3D scene. In FUI Earth mode, wait for the Earth intro gate before initializing the basemap. The alternate standalone renderer is created by an explicit switch or the optional post-ready idle preparation below.

For runtime 3D/2D/overlay switches, retain the committed source view while preparing the requested destination. A basemap-ready event or an older overlay frame is insufficient. Forward the current administrative path before a cold standalone build, then prepare its complete frame at the shared host's actual layout dimensions and active-quality DPR before reveal. Center its projected administrative body in that viewport by translating camera and orbit target together, preserving the view angle, zoom distance and field of view. Keep unchanged mounted models and canvases for return switches. A newer view request, changed navigation or removal cancels an obsolete reveal wait; it must never expose a stale scope.

When the FUI wrapper is active and its default-enabled view selector is available, a ready 3D/overlay source can prepare one alternate renderer after a 150 ms delay and an idle callback. A 3D source finishes its entrance first. This uses the same matching-scope, final-viewport, one-shot preparation; it changes neither the visible view nor loading UI and starts no hidden continuous RAF. View/navigation changes, deactivation, explicit disabling of the selector or removal cancel the idle request. A per-target navigation/style/color/quality signature avoids repeated warming, with bounded navigation rescheduling. Retaining both prepared renderers costs a second context and additional CPU/GPU resources and memory; it trades those resources for reusable future switches. A switch made before preparation finishes still waits for the real target draw.

Forward the initial vector destination's real-frame readiness through the Earth wrapper. After the parent's loading cover is removed by Vue, request one MapLibre repaint on the next frame so a settled map is visible without pointer input. This bounded presentation step does not create a continuous render loop.

## Evidence

Record `data-overlay-diagnostics` and `window.__SUMMER_MAP_OVERLAY_DIAGNOSTICS__`: shared canvas/context/camera, real feature/triangle counts, navigation/projection and errors, plus `quality`, `floatTargetsSupported`, `pmremActive`, `reflectionEnabled`, `glassMode`, `glassBloom`, `glassFallbackReason`, `hdrSamples`, `hdrSize`, `buildPhase` and `pendingNavigationKey`. For drilldown distinguish `buildStartedAtMs`, `modelPreparedAtMs` and `firstFrameAtMs` from `regionalRequestStartedAtMs` and `regionalReadyAtMs`; `terrainBlend` exposes actual progress from 0 to 1, and `regionalAppliedBeforeFirstFrame` identifies detail already complete in the first replacement draw. A `ready` event must follow actual overlay drawing, not merely basemap load. For view switches also inspect the destination navigation key, final viewport, submitted frame count and unchanged model identity on a cached return. Default glass mode keeps backdrop captures at zero. For opt-in transmission also inspect capture size/count/encoding and phase errors. Validate high/balanced appearance, low-budget behavior, actual LDR/direct fallback, oblique picking, replacement cancellation and non-null borrowed-FBO restoration on real WebGL. Existing screenshots and CPU tests do not establish cross-device frame rates or identical fallback fidelity.

Measure the whole navigation interval: input/model preparation, the real MapLibre `movestart` through `moveend`, and a complete post-flight tail of at least 500 ms. Keep regional-detail completion as a separate milestone. Record phase-specific RAF intervals, long tasks and native render/draw-call observations, retaining regressions and cache/network limits. A faster first ready frame does not establish a smooth camera transition; RAF callback cadence is not GPU presentation time or display FPS.
