# FUI Earth

## Purpose

A reusable, interactive geographic entrance for visualization applications. Click China to enter the administrative map, then return to Earth. Amber, glacier and alert palettes share the same scene structure and interaction.

## Design Principles

- Use real geographic geometry and preserve Earth-to-map navigation.
- Keep lighting and atmospheric falloff coherent around the entire sphere.
- Maintain consistent structural detail and visual hierarchy across palettes.
- Keep the instrumentation precise and restrained.

## Accessibility

Preserve keyboard-accessible preview controls and reduced-motion behavior. Decorative HUD remains pointer-transparent and aria-hidden.
